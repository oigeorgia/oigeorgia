title: "This is you"
---


This is you  
 molecules that for millennia hung together  
 so closely to form boundaries  
 fatty acids arranged to repel water  
 to establish a self and the world  
 inside the membrane,  
 genes that code for  
 proteins that form structures  
 and perform functions  
 receptors that sense  
 enzymes that transform  
 transporters that inform  
cells, that themselves are specialized to  
clean toxins, in the liver  
contract rhythmically, in the heart  
eliminate uncertainty, as immunity  
divide to sum, as in tissue growth  
divide to create, as in reproduction  
cells that generate electric current, as in the brain  
neuronal cells speaking to one another through little holes  
neurons that are excited by light  
by movement, force, touch  
by vibrations of the tiniest bone in the body  
by chemicals hanging in the air and inside our mouths  
by my little finger hitting the edge of the bed  
information that rushes and reaches  
often competes with other appeals  
it exists while it matters  
through the channels  
and the architecture of the brain  
neurons that formulate a code  
often temporary,  
importantly persistent  
through time and guidance  
specialized regions,  
built to last, breaks apart  
and integrates  
information.  
as a whole a brain,  
which I do not have  
but I am,  
that senses and integrates and thinks decides plans and finally guides  
a body that behaves  
to act to infer  
to respond to resolve  
its current madness  
unmatched, unpredictable accounts  
of the mundane  
tips the edge  
chaos  
organized chaos  
energy is free sometimes  
it can be used to create information  
like words never written before  
not in the past, although scarred for life  
not in the future, although constantly obsessed,  
not all the other possible states  
not anything in particular  
but everything as it is composed  
This is me.
